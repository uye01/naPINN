**Q2, your ablation.** Keeping warm-up and the EBM and freezing only the gate --
cutoff and steepness at their initial values, rejection cost zero -- gives rMAE
0.675 / 0.514 / 0.351 on Allen-Cahn / Burgers / lambda-omega at 15%, against
0.092 / 0.082 / 0.071 for full naPINN. The gate statistics show why: frozen, it
excludes 20.9-28.3% of the corrupted scalars; trained, 99.3-99.4%, with clean
rejection essentially unchanged (0.5-1.3% versus 0.7-1.1%). The estimator ranks
the corruption either way, as you anticipated; what has to be learned is where
to cut.
